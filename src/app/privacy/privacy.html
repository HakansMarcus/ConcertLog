Privacy Policy

ConcertLog is currently in beta. This policy explains what information is stored when you use the app.

What data is collected

When you sign in with Google, ConcertLog may receive your name, email address, profile picture, and unique user ID.

The app also stores the concert entries you create, including artist name, date, venue, city, festival, comments, and uploaded concert photos.

How your data is used

Your data is used to provide your personal concert archive, statistics, maps, planned concerts, and photo galleries.

Data storage

Concert entries are stored in Firebase Firestore. Uploaded photos are stored in Firebase Storage. Each user’s data is stored under their own account.

Who can access your data

Your concert data is private to your account inside the app. The app administrator may access stored data for maintenance, support, security, or bug fixing.

Uploaded content

Please avoid uploading sensitive, private, illegal, or copyrighted content that you do not have the right to use.

Data deletion

You may request deletion of your account data by contacting the app administrator. A self-service delete account feature may be added later.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact the app administrator.
